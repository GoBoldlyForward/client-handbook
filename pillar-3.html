section 3-2

Pioneer Process

While we can't predict the future, yet, we have a process for helping shape it. If you think back to explorers throughout history, not only was their an entreprenuerial spirit that drove them forward but there was also a similar process. We call that the Pioneer Process.

The Pioneer Process provides no answers, rather a framework to illuminate the journey ahead.

  1. Set a Target
  2. Plan the Approach
  3. Execute the Plan
  4. Reflect and Recalibrate
  5. Repeat

1. Target

Where are you headed?

Defining what success looks like is the cornerstone of progress. The destination will change and evolve, this is certain, but naming your target gives you something to anchor to and plan towards. Setting a target includes both where you are headed and why are you doing the work.

2. Plan

How will you get there?

It’s not just about where you’re headed but how you plan on getting there. The key elements to any pursuit that should be considered and optimized.

  • Timeline. How long do have or need?
  • Resources. What money, tools, and supplies are available or required?
  • People. Who will be able to help?

3. Execute

Take action.

With a clear target and a solid plan, it’s time to take action. Things will go wrong. Parts or all of the plan may be rendered useless. What matters is experience and adaptability.

4. Reflect

What did you learn?

Without reflection, growth is stifled and process is limited. Assessing the journey’s progress provides more perspective that can lead to compelling insights.

5. Repeat

Where are you headed next?

Once you’ve reflected on the journey thus far, it’s time to set a new destination. Our team works through this process in 2 week sprints. Before taking action again, we determine where we are headed and plan the approach.

section 3-3

Project Paths

At the core, we have three services: Strategy, Design, and Development. Within each there are consistent deliverables you can expect to see.

Strategy

Among our team, there are decades of unique experiences within technology. From creating products, building teams, and managing tradeoffs our strategic vision distills a project into it's key components and helps inform deliberate decision making. If you look to the Pioneer Process, you'll notice 75% involves drawing on knowledge, expertise, and learnings. When you plan well, building seems to take care of itself.

Design

More than graphics and pixels, think of design as the full experience. How it looks, feels, and flows are all part of design. To add clarity, design is commonly devided into two parts User Experience Design and Graphic Design.

UX

“How will customers use it?”

It's blueprints to the house. It will outline the end-to-end user experience of the application. To build a user experience we push deeper into the core functionality and map out the actual user flow.

Graphic Design

“What will customers see and feel?”

It's picking the colors, lighting, and textures for a home. It will outline the design system of the application and achieve decisions around colors, imagery, and aesthetic.

Development

“Is it real?”

It's putting up the walls and actually building the home. Combining the clarity of the UX and the visual identify of Design, Development is about creating a functioning product.

section 3-5

Get Started

Scope of Work

A friendly face will collaborate with you to create an SOW* for each segment of work. The SOW outlines the nuts and bolts of the work to be done which includes:

  • Deliverables
  • Estimated Timeline
  • Team Members

Once complete, you'll receive an email via Docusign to agree.

*SOW stands for Scope of Work

Actual Dates

Since our team members focus on 1 project at a time, locking in actual start dates for a project is done once commitment is received. Commitment for a project includes signed SOW and payment.

section 3-6

Doing the Work

Daily Stand-ups

Every day, the team working on your project meets to discuss progress on your project. Though this is an internal meeting, it ensures we maintain a tight feedback loop about project progress and any needs that emerge.

Weekly Check-in

While you'll hear from us throughout the week, we pause each Friday to send a summary on the main items that were completed as well as circling back on any needs.

Demo

The Demo is our chance to show you what we've been working on. It's the culminating event for each segment of work and allows you to review the progress. Following the Demo, is your Quality Assurance period.

Quality Assurance

The Quality Assurance, or QA, period is 2 per week of work. It's where everyone reviews and confirms that the SOW had been completed successfully. During this time, new areas typically emerge which can be added to the next phase or saved to be addressed later.

QA has 1 primary goal, confirm completion of SOW.
