Ch. 2 Method

Collaboration, communication, and doing great work together.

section 1-1

We believe in solutions.

Above all else, our goal is to help you solve problems. As a team of designers and engineers, we love asking big questions and bringing answers to life. Creating remarkable experiences for you and your audience is a natural result.

section 1-2

We pursue excellence, not perfection.

Whether it's the work we do or the way we do it, quality is at the forefront of our minds. Innovation doesn't have a finish line, so we learn fast and improve constantly.

  • We produce quality products.
  • We learn from our mistakes.
  • We improve continually.
  • We validate before we theorize.

section 1-3

We create remarkable experiences.

Our primary motivation is to provide uncommon value for our team, our clients, and their customers. It all comes down to honor, respect, and communication.

What does remarkable look like?

  • Our team members experience fulfilling work.
  • Our clients experience an enjoyable process and quality product.
  • Their customers experience valuable engagement.

section 1-4

We are effectively human.

There's a tension in programing cold, rigid machines for living, breathing people. Efficiency and productivity are important, but aren’t the goal. We engineer solutions that amplify human experiences.

  • We lead with a human-centered perspective.
  • We engineer thoughtful, simple solutions.
  • We engage in constructive debate.
  • We empathize before we optimize.

section 1-5

We go boldly forward.

Courageous, forward movement is key to innovating and doing work that matters. We push ourselves to improve and invite others along for the journey.

  • We default to action.
  • We make decisions.
  • We nurture a humble curiosity.
  • We take risks.
  • We bring others with us.
